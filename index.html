The problem we're solving

Kids are exposed to explicit content younger than ever, often by accident through the apps they already use every day. It can happen in a moment, and the experience may stay with them long after.

Early exposure can affect a developing child and, for some, grow into compulsive habits that undermine focus, relationships, and wellbeing.

Most parental tools today — bans, basic filters, browser-only settings — are easy to bypass. That leaves a real gap between the protection parents want and the risk their family still faces.
